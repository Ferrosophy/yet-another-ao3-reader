YAFR is a third-party [Archive of Our Own](https://archiveofourown.org) (AO3) reader built with SwiftUI and designed for iOS 26's Liquid Glass. Browse, search and save works offline, and read fanworks in any language with each translation shown right below its original paragraph.

> This is an unofficial third-party app and is not affiliated with AO3 or the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW).

## Features

### Reading and translation
- **Immersive translation**: the original stays; each paragraph's translation appears below it, or switch to translation only. Titles, tags, summaries and author's notes can be translated too, and you choose what's translated on the browse and reader pages.
- **Many translation services**:
  - No key needed: Google, Bing, Tencent TranSmart, Volcano, Baidu, Youdao, Sogou, DeepL (web) and Apple Translate (on device).
  - With your own API key: Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, DeepL API, OpenAI-compatible services (OpenAI / DeepSeek / OpenRouter / Groq / custom), Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini.
- **Reading modes**: scrolling or paging (curl, slide, cover, fade). Author's notes appear at the start or end of a chapter, just like on AO3.
- **Appearance**: dark mode, accent colors, custom backgrounds (color or image) and text colors; font size, weight, line, paragraph and letter spacing, and all four margins. Import TTF / OTF fonts, including from Google Drive or Baidu Netdisk.

### Browsing and search
- **Browse**: the latest works plus Kudos, comment and hit charts, with filters such as language.
- **Search**: works, tags, people and collections, with autocomplete suggestions and search history.
- **Detailed search and filters**: the same fields as AO3's Work Search and Sort and Filter, such as fandom, rating, warnings, category, characters, relationships, included / excluded tags, word count, update date, hits and kudos.

### Offline
- Works you add to **Offline** download the original first, then get translated, so you can read without a connection. If the work is already in your target language, only the original is downloaded.
- Failed downloads resume automatically. You can pause, retry or fetch only new chapters.
- Share as a bilingual EPUB generated by the app, or as AO3's AZW3 / MOBI / PDF / HTML. Works restricted to logged-in users can only be read offline in the app; they can't be exported or shared.
- Checking for new chapters only reads the chapter index, so it doesn't add hits to the work.

### Account and data
- **AO3 account**: after logging in, use your inbox, subscriptions, history, Marked for Later and bookmarks, and bookmark or subscribe to works, authors and series right in the app.
- **On this device**: without logging in, keep bookmarks, Marked for Later, history and subscriptions locally, or import them from AO3.
- **Sync**: your offline list, reading progress, reading time, preferences and search history sync across devices via iCloud or WebDAV.
- **Reading time**: statistics, plus a reminder to take a break after reading for a while.

### Connectivity
- **Mirrors**: add mirror sites that share AO3's structure. After you switch, all browsing, reading and logging in go through that source. **The app doesn't include or provide any mirror addresses.**
- **Onboarding**: on first launch the app checks whether AO3 can be reached. If not, it walks you through adding a mirror and suggests translation services that work in your region.
- **Human checks**: when Cloudflare or Anubis asks for verification, a verification page opens and returns you to the app once you pass.

### Interface languages
Traditional Chinese (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau), Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Hindi and Arabic.

## Installation

Requires an iPhone running **iOS 26 or later**. The app is distributed only through this repository's GitHub Releases; it isn't on the App Store or TestFlight.

1. Download `YAFR-<version>-unsigned.ipa` from [Releases](https://github.com/Ferrosophy/yet-another-ao3-reader/releases/latest).
2. **Verify the checksum**: each release lists the SHA-256 and includes a matching `.sha256` file. Run `shasum -a 256 YAFR-<version>-unsigned.ipa` (macOS / Linux) or `Get-FileHash YAFR-<version>-unsigned.ipa` (Windows PowerShell); the result must match.
3. **Sign and install** the unsigned IPA with your own Apple ID using [AltStore](https://altstore.io) or [SideStore](https://sidestore.io) (open the IPA with it on your iPhone), [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) (on a computer), or your own paid developer certificate.
4. Before first launch, trust your developer certificate in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, and turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security.

**Limits and risks**
- With a free Apple ID the app must be re-signed every **7 days** (AltStore / SideStore can refresh in the background), and you can have at most 3 self-signed apps installed.
- Signing tools need your Apple ID. Use tools you trust; a separate Apple ID just for signing is a good idea.
- **Only download the IPA from this repository's Releases.** Repackaged or "no-signing-needed" builds from elsewhere may contain malware or steal your AO3 account and API keys.
- To update, install the new IPA over the old one; local data is kept as long as you sign with the same Apple ID.

> **Logging in through a mirror is risky**: a mirror is not the official AO3 site, and whoever runs it can see the username and password you enter and the contents of your account. The app's connection test only checks that pages are compatible; it doesn't mean a mirror is trustworthy. Log in on the official site when possible, use only mirrors you trust, and don't reuse passwords. Only HTTPS mirrors are accepted.

## Privacy

- The app has no account system and no server, and it collects no usage data.
- Your AO3 login is kept as cookies on your device. Your password is never stored.
- Translation API keys stay in the device keychain and never sync to iCloud. Text is sent directly to the translation service you choose.
- Synced data is stored only in your own iCloud or WebDAV.

## Translation services

- **Text goes to the provider**: with translation on, the text to translate (body, titles, tags, summaries, notes and comments) is sent to the service you choose and handled under its privacy policy. **Only Apple Translate runs on device.** The app also tells you this the first time you turn on cloud translation in the reader.
- **Unofficial endpoints**: services marked "key-free" or "web" (Google, Bing, Tencent TranSmart, Volcano, Baidu, Youdao, Sogou, DeepL without a key) use unofficial web endpoints the providers haven't authorized. They may violate the providers' terms, be rate-limited or stop working. For reliable, compliant use, choose an official API (Google Cloud Translation, Microsoft Azure, DeepL API, OpenAI-compatible services, Claude, Gemini; bring your own key) or Apple Translate.
- **Author wishes**: if a work's tags, summary or notes say "do not translate", "No AI" and the like, the app doesn't translate it by default; you must confirm to translate it for your own reading, **AI translation services are never used** for it, and it isn't translated automatically when saved offline.
- **Accuracy**: machine translation can be wrong and doesn't replace the original. Rely on the original especially for tags, Archive Warnings and author's notes.

## Feedback and maintenance

- **Bugs and suggestions**: **Settings › Feedback** opens the bug report form in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ferrosophy/yet-another-ao3-reader/issues) with your version filled in; use the feature request template for ideas.
- **Security issues**: don't post them publicly; report privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Creators**: if you write on AO3 and have concerns about how your work is shown, translated or saved offline in this app, use the creator concern template. The app doesn't host or repost any work, and it respects AO3's "registered users only" setting.
- **Debug log**: if a mirror doesn't work, triple-tap **App Version** in Settings to open the debug page. Passwords, cookies and tokens are hidden, but the log still contains URLs you visited, so share it privately.
- **Expectations**: this is a personal, spare-time project. Issues are answered and fixed on a best-effort basis, without guaranteed response times; adapting to AO3 site changes may take a while.

If you enjoy the app, you can buy the developer a coffee in **Settings › Support Us**.

Donations support only the developer of this app. They are not affiliated with AO3 or the OTW and don't unlock anything; every feature is free forever. To support AO3 itself, please donate on the [OTW website](https://www.transformativeworks.org).

## Known issues

- The IPA is unsigned and must be signed yourself; with a free Apple ID it must be re-signed every 7 days.
- Requires iOS 26 or later.
- Some mirrors' protection (Cloudflare or Anubis) may still reject the app's requests after verification. Switch to another mirror if that happens.
- Key-free translation services use unofficial endpoints and may be rate-limited or temporarily unavailable.
- Comments can be read and translated but not posted; post comments on the original site.
- Some newly added interface text currently appears in English in languages other than Chinese and English.

## Backup and migration

- **New device**: turn on Settings › Sync (iCloud or WebDAV) on the old device and choose the same method on the new one to restore your offline list, reading progress, reading time, preferences, search history and on-device bookmarks.
- **Offline files** aren't synced; the new device re-downloads them from your offline list. Exported EPUBs and other files can be backed up from the Files app under YAFR › Bookshelf.
- **Not synced**: translation API keys, imported fonts and background images, translation cache and AO3 login state. Set these up or log in again after moving.
- The app's local data is included in iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone and is restored with them (except the translation cache).

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't reach AO3**: add a mirror you trust in Settings › Content Source, and complete any human check that appears.
- **AO3 rate limiting (HTTP 429)**: the app stops retrying and shows how long to wait.
- **Pages fail to load or look incomplete**: AO3 may have changed its pages. Triple-tap App Version in Settings to open the debug page, copy the log and report it in Issues so the parser can be updated. Works already saved offline aren't affected.
- **Translation fails**: switch services in Settings › Translation Service (for example, on-device Apple Translate). Reading the original is unaffected.
- **Offline reading** works without a connection.
