YAAO3R is a third-party [Archive of Our Own](https://archiveofourown.org) (AO3) reader built with SwiftUI and designed for iOS 26's Liquid Glass. Browse, search and save works offline, and read fanworks in any language with each translation shown right below its original paragraph.

> This is an unofficial third-party app and is not affiliated with AO3 or the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW).

## Features

### Reading and translation
- **Immersive translation**: the original stays; each paragraph's translation appears below it, or switch to translation only. Titles, tags, summaries and author's notes can be translated too, and you choose what's translated on the browse and reader pages.
- **Many translation services**:
  - No key needed: Google, Bing, Tencent TranSmart, Volcano, Baidu, Youdao, Sogou, DeepL (web) and Apple Translate (on device).
  - With your own API key: Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, DeepL API, OpenAI-compatible services (OpenAI / DeepSeek / OpenRouter / Groq / custom), Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini.
- **Reading modes**: scrolling or paging (curl, slide, cover, fade). Author's notes appear at the start or end of a chapter, just like on AO3.
- **Appearance**: dark mode, accent colors, custom backgrounds (color or image) and text colors; font size, weight, line, paragraph and letter spacing, and all four margins. Import TTF / OTF fonts, including from Google Drive or Baidu Netdisk.

### Browsing and search
- **Browse**: the latest works plus Kudos, comment and hit charts, with filters such as language.
- **Search**: works, tags, people and collections, with autocomplete suggestions and search history.
- **Detailed search and filters**: the same fields as AO3's Work Search and Sort and Filter, such as fandom, rating, warnings, category, characters, relationships, included / excluded tags, word count, update date, hits and kudos.

### Offline
- Works you add to **Offline** download the original first, then get translated, so you can read without a connection. If the work is already in your target language, only the original is downloaded.
- Failed downloads resume automatically. You can pause, retry or fetch only new chapters.
- Share as a bilingual EPUB generated by the app, or as AO3's AZW3 / MOBI / PDF / HTML.

### Account and data
- **AO3 account**: after logging in, use your inbox, subscriptions, history, Marked for Later and bookmarks, and bookmark or subscribe to works, authors and series right in the app.
- **On this device**: without logging in, keep bookmarks, Marked for Later, history and subscriptions locally, or import them from AO3.
- **Sync**: your offline list, reading progress, reading time, preferences and search history sync across devices via iCloud or WebDAV.
- **Reading time**: statistics, plus a reminder to take a break after reading for a while.

### Connectivity
- **Mirrors**: add mirror sites that share AO3's structure. After you switch, all browsing, reading and logging in go through that source. **The app doesn't include or provide any mirror addresses.**
- **Onboarding**: on first launch the app checks whether AO3 can be reached. If not, it walks you through adding a mirror and suggests translation services that work in your region.
- **Human checks**: when Cloudflare or Anubis asks for verification, a verification page opens and returns you to the app once you pass.

### Interface languages
Traditional Chinese (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau), Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Hindi and Arabic.

## Installation

- Requires an iPhone running **iOS 26 or later**.
- Download `YAAO3R-<version>-unsigned.ipa` from [Releases](https://github.com/Ferrosophy/yet-another-ao3-reader/releases/latest).
- The IPA is **unsigned**. Sign it with [AltStore](https://altstore.io), [SideStore](https://sidestore.io), [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) or your own developer certificate before installing.
- Apps signed with a free Apple ID must be re-signed every 7 days.

> Your password is sent to the selected source when you log in, so only use mirrors you trust.

## Privacy

- The app has no account system and no server, and it collects no usage data.
- Your AO3 login is kept as cookies on your device. Your password is never stored.
- Translation API keys stay in the device keychain and never sync to iCloud. Text is sent directly to the translation service you choose.
- Synced data is stored only in your own iCloud or WebDAV.

## Feedback

**Settings › Feedback** opens [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ferrosophy/yet-another-ao3-reader/issues) with your version information filled in. If a mirror doesn't work, triple-tap **App Version** in Settings to open the debug page, then copy the log and attach it.

If you enjoy the app, you can buy the developer a coffee in **Settings › Support Us**.

## Development

Requires Xcode 26 or later and the iOS 26 SDK.

```bash
brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate
open AO3Reader.xcodeproj
```

- Dependency: [SwiftSoup](https://github.com/scinfu/SwiftSoup) (Swift Package Manager, HTML parsing).
- The spec is in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) (Chinese); the localization pipeline is in [scripts/l10n](scripts/l10n/README.md).
- `.github/workflows/build-ipa.yml` builds an **unsigned** IPA on GitHub Actions. Pushing a `v1.2.3` tag, or running it manually with "release" checked, also publishes a GitHub Release.

## Notes

- Please follow AO3's [Terms of Service](https://archiveofourown.org/tos). The app only loads pages when you act, and it stops retrying and shows the wait time when it receives HTTP 429.
- The key-free translation services use unofficial endpoints and may be rate-limited or change. For reliability, use a service with your own API key.
- Works belong to their authors. Please don't redistribute downloaded or exported works or their translations.
